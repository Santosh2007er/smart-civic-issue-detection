Categorized Infrastructure Issues

Automated Detection for Key Municipal Hazards

Our pre-trained vision engine is calibrated to detect, categorize and prioritize critical urban infrastructure defects.

Potholes

Deep asphalt cavities and crater formations causing high-impact vehicular damage and safety hazards for two-wheelers.

Priority: High SLA: 48 Hrs

Garbage Accumulation

Overflowing dumpsters, unauthorized waste heaps, and scattered plastic debris posing public health hazards.

Priority: Medium / High SLA: 24 Hrs

Damaged Roads

Extensive alligator cracking, broken pavement borders, loose aggregate, and subgrade foundation deterioration.

Priority: Medium SLA: 72 Hrs

Broken Streetlights

Non-functional nocturnal luminaires, dangling electrical wiring, and dark corridor risk zones along pedestrian walkways.

Priority: Medium SLA: 24 Hrs
How It Works

From Photo to Resolution in 4 Simple Steps

Bridging the gap between citizen reporting and municipal field action through intelligent automation.

1. Upload Photo

Take or upload a picture of the civic issue directly from your phone or browser.

2. AI Detection

The deep learning model identifies problem category, confidence score & severity.

3. Geolocation Pin

GPS automatically tags the precise coordinates on OpenStreetMap with Leaflet.

4. Track & Resolve

Monitor resolution through 4 transparent milestones from submission to completion.
